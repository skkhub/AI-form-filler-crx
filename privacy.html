AI FORM FILLER

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

Thank you for using AI Form Filler (the “Extension”). We value your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Extension.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide: To use the Extension’s core features, such as intelligent form filling, you may need to enter custom settings, such as an API key for a third-party AI service. This information is stored only in your local browser storage (such as chrome.storage.local).

Form and page data: When you initiate a form-filling action, the Extension may read the structure or text content of the current webpage to generate AI-powered filling suggestions.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected, including API keys you provide and form content read temporarily, is used only to provide form automation and AI-powered form-filling features on your device.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data, form content, or API keys.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your settings, such as API keys, are stored locally in your browser by default. We do not upload them to a separate backend server operated by the developer.

When the Extension communicates with a large language model API, relevant content is encrypted in transit.

4. Permissions

Permissions required by the Extension, such as access to webpage content, are used only to provide its features. They are not used to track users or collect unrelated private information.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page and take effect on the date of publication.
